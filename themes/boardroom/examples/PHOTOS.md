## Fonts

The theme uses only PowerPoint-safe system fonts and bundles no font files.

| Family | Role | Availability | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Georgia | action titles, essay, pull quotes, narrative numerals | ships with macOS and Windows; present in Office installations | Microsoft core font; no file is redistributed here |
| Arial | kickers, chart labels, owners, dates, units, footers | ships with macOS and Windows; present in Office installations | Monotype; no file is redistributed here |

No webfont is required for delivery. The optional preview renders use the same two faces. If a project
requires a different editorial serif or a condensed kicker, resolve it with the project identity and
confirm the licence and target availability before use; do not substitute a display face that breaks
the exported file.

## Demonstration content

All company names, figures, scenarios, owners and dates in the example pages are illustrative and
constructed for this template. They are not sourced facts and must be replaced or labelled as
demonstration data in a real document.
