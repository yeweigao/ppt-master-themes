# Brand Geometry — composition references

Read the portable Style for the method. These are optional constructions; final geometry belongs to the project plan and source SVG.

## Extract before decorating

Inspect the actual identity. Identify contour, principal angle, stroke-to-shape ratio, gap interval, repetition and overlap. Choose the recognizable parts that survive at different sizes; preserve official artwork separately. Distinguish sampled logo color from earlier confirmed identity colors and place both in the project's palette evidence when relevant.

## Frame field

Enlarge an open contour near a page edge. Pair it with one offset contour or a small repeated marker so the family remains recognizable. Leave the main reading field quiet. Crop derived decoration deliberately when useful; do not crop the formal logo to achieve it.

## Line rhythm

Use a small bundle of parallel strokes at the identity's angle and interval. Repeat or extend the bundle as an edge band, chapter marker or divider. Align its endpoints to the grid where possible. Do not use decorative strokes as ambiguous information connectors.

## Gradient + motif

Place a soft field of resolved identity colors behind a larger, lower-contrast frame fragment or sparse related shapes. Preserve a clean prose field and stronger motif detail outside it. Keep the gradient and geometry editable when the host supports them; inspect the exported result because gradient mapping may differ between renderers.

## Recognition check

Before enlarging or fragmenting a mark, resolve its actual connection and crossing structure, endpoints, overlaps, proportions and negative spaces. Compare the source and proposed motif visually at intended size. Two designs may contain the same squares and lines while having entirely different identities. A recognizable geometric vocabulary is an earned result, not a consequence of listing the source's primitives. When fragments fail this check, use the complete supplied mark or independent atmospheric geometry; do not fill the page with arbitrary shapes and label them brand-derived.
