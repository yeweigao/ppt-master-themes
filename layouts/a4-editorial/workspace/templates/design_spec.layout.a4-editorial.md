# A4 Editorial — Design Specification

## IV. Signature Design Elements

### Reading field, six-track Swiss alignment, and four-column modularity

The interior field spans x=100…1140. Six 140-unit tracks are separated by 40-unit gutters. A 3+3 composition gives two 500-unit columns; 4+2 gives 680-unit main text and a 320-unit side note; a symmetric four-module division inside the same outer margins (`x=100, 370, 640, 910`, width 230, gutter 40) shares the outer and center axes for four-card matrices, metric headers and staggered pillars. Tracks are alignment guides, not six narrow prose columns. Reading pages reserve y=100…1610; bottom folios sit at y=1650. Headlines may span tracks while paragraphs retain their assigned measure.

On the `1240 × 1754` A4 authoring canvas (`210 × 297 mm` = `595.28 × 841.89 pt`, where `1 SVG unit ≈ 0.48 pt`), prototypes follow a calibrated Swiss International Typographic Scale so reading flow matches information importance: hero/display `84–300 px` Bold, page titles `56–84 px` Bold, framing leads `28–34 px` Regular (visibly larger than body copy), sustained 2-column prose `22–26 px` Regular (`10.6–12.5 pt` print), compact module/sidebar notes `19–22 px` Regular, kickers/specimen ticks `16–18 px` Bold tracked uppercase, and a strict physical floor of `14–16 px` (`6.7–7.7 pt` print; never `< 14 px`). Prototypes use neutral preview paint, a safe face and provisional sizes only. Final identity and reading typography belong to the applied project. Test real-language line lengths and printed size before filling capacity: the 500-unit columns are intended for sustained prose at a close-reading scale, while the 320-unit sidecar holds short notes. The one-column side-photo variant is deliberately short. Overflow moves to a suitable sibling or another page rather than reducing the recurring body scale. The rows below are capacities, not required content or a narrative sequence.

### Image zones and trim

Image fields touch the indicated trim edges or sit inside crisp structural borders. Cover/chapter have overlay-title safe zones; other photographs keep captions on quiet ground or a narrow local scrim. Crops are selected from the actual subject position, with people and meaningful context kept intact. Reading page edges do not force every image into an inset rectangle. The prototypes establish trim-size composition; a printer's extra bleed/trim marks are separate output requirements.

The two panorama layouts form one 2480×1240 photograph field across their top zones. Select one source and one common scale/crop before splitting its normalized source rectangle at the midpoint. Use the left crop on an even page and the right crop on its facing odd page. Keep important text, faces and focal details clear of the fold; never crop the halves independently. The roster order is a catalog, not booklet pagination. A single-image substitute uses another Layout.

### Native structure and flexible content

All prototypes select Master `a4-editorial` / `A4 Editorial`, which owns only a common neutral background. Layouts own fixed separators; page copy, images and folios stay editable in typed slots. Picture carriers contain a neutral one-pixel preview, replaceable downstream. Each body carrier enters its complete frame at the upper left. Title, lead, body, note, caption and folio remain distinct semantic roles.

The open-table prototype uses a composite object region: its light horizontal guides are a demonstration, not fixed row/column counts or table data. The generated project authors actual cells and native Table metadata from its real data. Four ordered step regions accept labels and short explanations; their vertical spine encodes sequence without imposing a particular subject. No example text, organization, color, font identity or document objective is part of this Layout.

### Rhythm, avant-garde fashion editorial, and deconstructivist variants

The extended roster (`03j`–`03z`) combines **Swiss International Typographic Order** with **Avant-Garde Fashion Editorial pacing**, **Deconstructivist cropping/slicing**, and **restrained Neo-Brutalist structural honesty**:

- **Specimen coordinate rules & masthead matrices (`03u`, `03v`)**: Top horizontal rules with calibrated tick marks establish a typographic specimen or lookbook header above full-width display wordmarks. In `03v_content_matrix`, the masthead governs four alternating vertical modules—two crisp hairline-framed portraits paired with two solid editorial cards (featuring linear stripe rhythm and watermark geometry).
- **Hero metrics & off-axis masonry (`03m`, `03w`)**: `03m` provides a classic six-photo bounded masonry; `03w_content_metrics_masonry` pairs a four-column oversized numeric header (`15+ / 24+ / 99+ / 32+` scale) with an eight-photo multi-column masonry whose upper edge is sliced by a subtle canted wedge (`#canted-top-wedge`), creating dynamic off-axis tension while keeping every picture carrier orthogonal and natively replaceable.
- **Die-cut archive plates & stepped geometric counterweights (`03p`, `03q`)**: `03p_content_overlay` places a Slide-local floating white archive plate with top and bottom semicircular die-cut notches over an expansive photograph, separating headline and body with an interior hairline. `03q_content_geometry` layers a circular counterweight, stepped architectural blocks and exposed `3px` portrait borders.
- **Deconstructed modular check mask (`03x`)**: `03x_content_deconstruct` overlays a full-bleed photograph with stepped solid and semi-translucent rectangular cells aligned to the grid, framing a diagonal central corridor for the photographic subject while seating editable copy and vector navigation cues (`↗`, `< >`) inside the solid blocks.
- **Offset manifesto, framed portrait & sliced display counter-form (`03y`)**: `03y_content_atelier` reserves generous upper negative space for an off-center manifesto statement and four-line micro-index, while the lower half contrasts a thick-bordered portrait (`stroke-width="8"`) with vertically stretched, horizontally sliced monolith bars and a negative-space counter-form cutout.
- **Inverted monolith field & staggered cropped-numeral pillars (`03z`)**: `03z_content_pillars` sets four white vertical pillars of unequal height and vertical start against a full-bleed dark ground. Each pillar clips an oversized serif numeral (`1`–`4`) at its lower-right edge while explanatory copy occupies the dark negative space beneath Pillars 2 and 4.

### Structural philosophy

This Layout carries the package's structural convictions so they travel with every document that installs it:

- **The grid is the ornament.** Order comes from shared edges, measured intervals, specimen rules and ticks; rounded cards, drop shadows and decorative panels are not part of the vocabulary.
- **Numbers are typography, not tiles.** Hero figures (`03w`) stand on rules with labels; there is no KPI tile, gauge, progress pill or icon-and-number card in the roster.
- **Rhythm over repetition.** The roster spans four temperatures — photographic, quiet reading, precise evidence and dark or deconstructed turning points — so a document can alternate attention demands. A repeated skeleton is a deliberate sequence of at most three pages that changes one variable.
- **Scale contrast is structural.** Display, chapter and figure slots are dimensioned for a large-to-small ratio of at least 1.6× against the reading size, so hierarchy survives a 20% thumbnail and grayscale printing.
- **Evidence keeps its route.** Evidence pages run title → exhibit → annotation → source along the grid, and a source line has a fixed home.
- **Photographs are subjects, not fillers.** They reach the trim they touch, continue across facing pages from one crop, and carry text only on a scrim, a solid cell or a plate.
